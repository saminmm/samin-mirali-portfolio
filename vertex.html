Context

Vertex is a unified video streaming and creator management platform designed to seamlessly integrate content consumption with professional content production.

The project aimed to simplify complex workflows for creators while enhancing discovery and engagement for viewers, delivering a scalable, user-centred product that balances intuitive design with professional-grade tools.

My Role & Responsibilities

I collaborated within a five-person design team, contributing across all stages of the project: research, strategy, information architecture, wireframes, prototyping, UI design, design system development, usability testing and final delivery.

I played an active role in shaping research insights, ideation and design decisions, ensuring the final product aligned with both strategic goals and user needs while maintaining a polished, professional-grade experience.

Product Objectives

  • Streamline content discovery and organization for viewers.
  • Simplify video upload and management workflows for creators.
  • Centralize analytics, monetization and copyright management in a single dashboard.
  • Reduce cognitive load through clean, intuitive interfaces.
  • Build a scalable design system to support future growth.
  • Improve transparency and usability of content performance insights.

Target Users

Content Creators

  • Need advanced analytics, actionable insights and clear monetization reporting.
  • Require efficient upload, management and copyright protection workflows.

Viewers

  • Seek effortless content discovery and seamless organization tools.
  • Expect intuitive navigation and engaging community interactions.

The six-week timeline included discovery and strategy, information architecture and UX, low-fidelity wireframing, design system and UI, prototyping and motion, and testing with final polishing.

Research Objective

The purpose of the research phase was to better understand how viewers and content creators interact with existing video platforms, and to identify friction points within navigation, content discovery, and creator workflows. The goal was to uncover actionable insights that could inform a more intuitive, streamlined and scalable platform experience for both user groups.

  • How users perceive platform navigation and information hierarchy.
  • How easily creators transition between viewer and creator modes.
  • The efficiency of current upload and content management workflows.
  • The clarity of creator dashboards and analytics tools.
  • The extent to which creators rely on external tools during content preparation.

Research Methodology

This methodology was an online survey supported by user interviews, competitive analysis, open-ended feedback collection and comparative workflow evaluation between viewer and creator experiences.

Participants included both frequent video consumers and semi-professional content creators.

  • Online user survey with viewers and creators.
  • User interviews to understand real behaviour and pain points.
  • Competitive analysis of major video platforms.
  • Qualitative open-ended feedback collection.
  • Comparative workflow evaluation between viewer and creator experiences.

Affinity Mapping

Interview insights were synthesized using an affinity diagram to group similar observations and identify recurring patterns.

This helped reveal the primary usability challenges and guided the definition of key design opportunities for Vertex.

  • Cluttered sidebar and unclear hierarchy make navigation difficult.
  • Dense layouts create cognitive overload.
  • Low discoverability of creator tools.
  • Publishing requires multiple steps and external tools.

Competitive Analysis

To better understand the current landscape of video-sharing platforms, I conducted a competitive analysis on several major platforms including YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Vimeo and Rumble.

The goal was to evaluate how these platforms structure their navigation systems, creator tools and content management features.

Competitive Observations

Competitive observations helped summarize the strengths and weaknesses of each platform across navigation, content experience and creator tools.

User Persona

The personas helped focus the design around two product paths: the Viewer Experience and the Creator Studio.

Design Opportunities

Based on research findings and persona needs, several key opportunities were identified to improve the experience for both viewers and content creators on video platforms.

Problem Statement

Video platforms offer powerful features for both viewers and creators, but these features are often presented through complex interfaces and inefficient workflows. As a result, viewers experience difficulty navigating content, while creators face challenges in accessing creator tools and managing the upload process. Vertex aims to address these issues by creating a simplified and intuitive platform that supports both seamless content discovery and efficient creator workflows.

How Might We Questions

The HMW questions translated research findings into focused design challenges that guided ideation and product decisions.

MVP Definition

Based on the research insights and HMW questions, the initial product scope was defined by identifying the most essential features required to support both viewers and content creators.

  • Simplified sidebar navigation.
  • Personalized video feed and content discovery.
  • Creator dashboard with basic analytics.
  • Streamlined video upload workflow.
  • Integrated editing tools during upload.
  • User channel and subscription system.

Feature Prioritisation

Features were prioritized using the MoSCoW framework to keep the first version focused, manageable and aligned with user needs.

Design Principles

  • Clarity First: interfaces should remain simple and easy to understand, reducing cognitive overload for both viewers and creators.
  • Creator Accessibility: creator tools should be clearly visible and easy to access within the platform.
  • Streamlined Workflows: key tasks such as uploading and managing content should require as few steps as possible.
  • Integrated Creation Tools: essential editing and publishing tools should be available within the platform to reduce reliance on external software.

Information Architecture

The platform structure was designed around two primary user paths: Viewer Experience for content discovery and video consumption, and Creator Studio for managing, uploading and analyzing content. Separating these experiences helps reduce interface complexity while keeping creator tools easily accessible.

Ideate Phase: Storyboard

The storyboard illustrates a typical creator scenario: Alex records a tutorial, needs editing tools, struggles with different platform formats, discovers Vertex, edits thumbnails and metadata inside the upload flow, and publishes without switching tools.

User Flow

The user flows mapped two journeys: discovering and watching a video as a viewer, and uploading and publishing content as a creator.

Low-Fidelity Wireframes

Wireframes explored the Home page, Video Player, Explore page, Creator Dashboard, Upload Workflow and Integrated Editor.

Design System

The Vertex design system was created to ensure visual consistency, usability and scalability across the platform. The system focuses on clarity, minimalism and accessibility to reduce cognitive overload while supporting both viewers and creators.

The palette uses a primary burgundy system with tints, shadows, neutrals and semantic colors.

Iconography
