Project 03 · Email & Visual Design

E-commerce Email Design

A collection of promotional email concepts created for fashion, skincare and automotive brands, focused on mobile-first layout, visual hierarchy, campaign storytelling and conversion clarity.

Goal

The goal was to design visually engaging e-commerce email campaigns that communicate offers quickly, guide users through content and encourage action through clear CTA placement.

Design Focus

Each concept explores a different brand personality: fashion urgency for Bershka, soft editorial skincare for Revolution Skin Care, and bold automotive energy for Tinybot.

Design Principles

The email layouts were designed around quick scanning, strong hero visuals, clear product sections and repeated call-to-action placement.

  • Clear hierarchy for titles, offer messages and CTA buttons.
  • Strong image-led storytelling to quickly communicate the brand mood.
  • Mobile-first composition with simple product grouping.
  • Consistent footer and social areas for trust and campaign completion.

Bershka Sale Email

A fashion promotion email focused on urgency, lifestyle imagery and a repeated “Shop the Sale” call to action.

Revolution Skin Care Email

A soft skincare routine email using editorial typography, calm spacing and product-focused storytelling.

Tinybot Automotive Email

A bold, high-contrast automotive campaign with strong imagery, product blocks and direct conversion points.
