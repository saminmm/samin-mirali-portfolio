About Me

During my studies, I completed a 9-month internship at the Apple Developer Academy in Naples, a multidisciplinary program focused on app design, collaborative problem solving and professional skills.

Since October 2024, I have worked as a freelance designer, building UX/UI and brand projects for clients across mobile, web and digital product experiences.
